# Privacy Policy for ThermalGlider 2D

Last updated: October 2026

ThermalGlider 2D respects your privacy. This policy describes how data is handled in the app.

## 1. Information We Collect
We do not collect any personal identity information such as names, email addresses, or precise locations.

## 2. Third-Party Services and Advertising
The app integrates Google AdMob and ironSource LevelPlay to display ads. These services may collect:
- Device identifiers (IDFA, Advertising ID)
- Coarse location data (country/city level)
- App performance and crash diagnostics

## 3. Children's Privacy
ThermalGlider 2D is a general audience arcade game and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.
